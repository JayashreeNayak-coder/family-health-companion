

# Family Health Companion
## Problem Statement

### 1. Background

Families often manage health information across multiple hospitals, laboratories, paper documents, prescriptions, and digital applications. This information is difficult to organize and retrieve when needed.

Family members may also struggle to remember medication details, past symptoms, previous medical visits, and relevant health history—particularly when managing the health records of children, elderly parents, or dependents.

### 2. Problem

There is no simple, centralized way for many families to maintain and access their health information over time.

Key problems include:

- Medical reports, scans, and prescriptions are scattered across different locations and formats.
- Previous medical history may be forgotten or unavailable during doctor visits.
- Medication schedules and adherence can be difficult to track.
- Food intake and symptoms are rarely recorded consistently.
- Families lack an organized timeline of health-related events.
- Preparing a concise health history for a medical appointment can be time-consuming.
- Recipe suggestions may not account for a family member’s known allergies or dietary restrictions.

### 3. Proposed Solution

Family Health Companion is a web-based application that helps individuals and families securely organize health records, track relevant health information, and access AI-assisted support.

The application will provide:

- Family profiles and health record management.
- Secure storage and organization of medical documents.
- A chronological health timeline.
- Medication and lab-result tracking.
- Food and symptom logging.
- AI-assisted document extraction with user review and approval.
- Record-based health summaries.
- General and health-aware recipe suggestions.
- An optional, user-controlled emergency health card.

### 4. Intended Users

- Individuals managing their own health records.
- Family members managing records for dependents or elderly relatives.
- Families who want easier access to organized health information.

### 5. Project Goal

The goal is to develop a functional, online prototype that demonstrates the core workflows and can be evaluated by college faculty and potential users.

The application will be built using maintainable software engineering practices so that it can evolve into a secure, commercially viable product based on user feedback.

### 6. Scope Boundary

Family Health Companion is a health-record organization and support application. It is **not** intended to diagnose diseases, prescribe treatment, predict medical outcomes, or replace qualified healthcare professionals.

AI-generated information will be clearly identified, grounded in available records, and subject to user review where appropriate.
