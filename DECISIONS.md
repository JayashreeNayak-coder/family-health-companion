| D-019 | 2026-11-05 | Made under D-018, for the owner to review. Phase 5 adds `family_invitation`, with a hashed single-use token for one email and a 7-day expiry, and `GET /families` and an invitation preview. Any member may create their own profile. An invitation can offer a profile, and accepting it is how an adult's account gets linked, which is where the adult chooses the access others keep (D-013). A linked adult's own `MANAGE` can't be removed. | `docs/05` had invitation endpoints but no table, and no way to list a user's families or to link a profile. Without their own profile, invited adults would depend on a manager, against D-013. |
| D-020 | 2026-11-06 | Made under D-018, for the owner to review. Documents can be attached to any health record or food log entry as evidence, through `record_attachment`, separate from a record's single source document. Categories add `BILL` and `PHOTO`, and documents get an optional description. | Object storage needs a provider and account, which wait on Phase 3. The owner asked for photos and files on records as proof of visits, medication, and scans. |
| D-021 | 2026-11-06 | AI provider: Google Gemini, chosen by the owner, behind `DocumentExtractor` so it can be replaced. Called with plain HTTP, adding no SDK. Real health data needs the paid tier or another provider first. | D-025 | 2026-11-09 | Made under D-018, for the owner to review. Phase 14 (general meal assistant): `ai.RecipeGenerator` is the provider seam, with prompt and schema `resources/ai/recipes/v1`. Recipes use temperature 0.7 for variety (extraction stays at 0). Each AI feature has its own daily count under the existing limits. "Meal ideas" is a top-level page at `/meals`, because general mode belongs to no profile. Ingredients the user lists to avoid are checked by loose text matching that only flags. No new dependencies. | Builds on D-021 and `docs/06` sections 8.2 and 8.5. |
